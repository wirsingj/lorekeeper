# Maintainer Guide
Adapt headings; omit unused sections; keep procedures actionable/current.
## Setup And Commands
Shortest checkout-to-work path, tools, assumptions. Separate executed/defined commands and unverified procedures. Retain consequential command, outcome, date, revision, environment; existence is not a pass.
## Focused Checks And Diagnostics
Test/build/lint/diagnostic procedures and interpretation; service/hardware/account/credential requirements without sensitive values. Retain sanitized outcomes, not logs. Name consequential invariants, independent expectation sources, comparison conditions, evidence gaps; matching outputs alone do not establish intent.
## Important And Dangerous Files
Editing boundaries and important files; no complete inventory.
## Failure, Release, And Recovery Procedures
Recurring symptoms, likely owners, evidence, recovery. Relevant release, rollback, backup/restore procedures and verification limits.
## YAIML Maintenance
Chosen memory home (default `.yaiml/`), actual discovery/shared-guide/catalog/template/YAIMLACP routes, supported host entries and activation gaps. Current reference source/revision, material dirty-reference changes, project-owned skills, local adaptations and per-resource exceptions after partial refresh; keep private reference locations in workspace configuration. Replace current notes rather than keeping an update log.
Ordinary work reads relevant context and maintains material understanding without reminders. Named and natural requests use the same local methods. Re-init/refresh reconciles source guidance with local knowledge and skills; healthy repeat runs remain unchanged. Relocation follows the saved Bootstrap procedure and scoped approval. Preserve unknown discovery fields and check actual consumers before explicit migration.
Keep contributor/reviewer responsibilities and current scoped coordination decisions, limits and cancellation gaps. Skill use does not authorize workers. Routine edits stay passage-scoped; before integration reconcile target/common ancestor and combined evidence. Broad cleanup preserves governed retention. Report memory counts, justified growth and overages in the task response.
